How to play

Buy your first particle with the button in the bottom-right, then swipe back and forth near falling particles to hit them — each swipe leaves a trail that pulls nearby particles toward it.

Swipe steadily back and forth and the yellow ×N indicator grows — release once it's yellow to hand control to the rhythm autopilot. Slower, more even swings reach a higher cap.

Tap the ×N indicator itself to discover the breath autopilot — lay the phone flat and breathe slowly instead of swiping.

A gentle side-to-side tilt — not lifting the phone up and down.

Score becomes Echo once atoms form and stay stable. Tap echo: N at the top to spend it, or to end the epoch once you're ready.

Recipes

Beyond swiping, particles can combine into something new — binding, fusing, or capturing an electron to form a stable atom. Each combination rewards its own bonus, and finishing atoms is how Echo is earned.
